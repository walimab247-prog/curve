Privacy Policy

What data this site does and does not collect, and how your preferences are stored.

Last updated: 2026-08-17

1. Overview

Curve Learn ("the Site") is a static educational website. We aim to collect as little data as possible. This policy explains what limited information is processed when you use the Site.

2. Data We Do Not Collect

  • We do not require accounts or registration.
  • We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.
  • We do not sell or share personal data with third parties.
  • We do not run third-party analytics scripts on this Site.

3. Local Storage

The Site stores a single value in your browser's local storage to remember that you dismissed the cookie notice. This value never leaves your device and can be removed at any time by clearing your browser data.

4. Contact Form and Email

The on-site contact form performs validation in your browser only and does not transmit data to a server. If you email us directly, we receive the information you choose to send (your address and message). We use it solely to respond and do not add you to any mailing list.

5. Hosting Logs

Our hosting provider may record standard technical logs (such as IP address, browser type, and requested pages) for security and operational purposes, subject to the provider's own policies.

6. External Links

Articles link to external sources such as protocol documentation. Those sites have their own privacy practices, which we do not control.

7. Changes and Contact

We will update this page when our practices change and revise the date above. Questions? See the Contact page.
