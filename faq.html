Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to common questions about Curve, CRV, and oracles.

What is Curve?

Curve is a decentralized exchange protocol optimized for trading between assets of similar value, such as stablecoins. It runs on Ethereum and several other blockchains and is governed by holders of its native token through an on-chain voting system.

What is CRV used for?

CRV is the protocol's governance token. Holders can lock it to vote on protocol parameters and the distribution of incentives. It is a utility and governance instrument within the ecosystem — this site does not evaluate it as an investment.

What are oracle risks?

The main risks are data manipulation (an attacker distorting the value a feed reports), stale data (feeds not updating in time during volatile markets), and centralization (too few independent operators or sources). Each can cause dependent contracts to act on incorrect information.

Is this investment advice?

No. All content on Curve Learn is educational and informational. We do not provide investment, financial, legal, or tax advice, and nothing here is a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any asset.

How are data feeds verified?

Typical designs aggregate values from many independent sources and operators, discard outliers, apply time-weighted averaging, and enforce on-chain freshness and deviation checks before contracts consume the data.
