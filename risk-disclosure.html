Curve Learn publishes educational material about DeFi protocols and oracle infrastructure. We do not operate any protocol, custody any assets, or facilitate transactions. Reading this site involves no financial exposure, but acting on any information you read anywhere may.

2. Digital Asset Risks

  • Total loss: the value of any crypto asset, including CRV, can fall to zero.
  • Volatility: prices can change dramatically within minutes.
  • Technology failure: smart contracts, bridges, and oracles can be exploited or malfunction.
  • Liquidity: you may be unable to exit a position at a fair price, or at all.
  • Custody: lost keys and phishing attacks can result in irreversible loss.
